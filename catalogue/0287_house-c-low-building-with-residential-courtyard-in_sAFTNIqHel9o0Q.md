## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Grid-based Spatial Definition: Utilize a consistent underlying grid to organize elements, ensuring visual coherence and structural integrity in complex layouts.
- Transparency for Contextual Integration: Employ transparent or semi-transparent elements to connect distinct functional zones, fostering a sense of openness and integrating internal activities with external environments.
- Minimalist Form for Functional Clarity: Design objects and spaces with essential forms, removing superfluous ornamentation to highlight function and material honesty.
- Hierarchical Line Weight for Information Prioritization: Vary line thickness to differentiate primary structural elements from secondary details or implied boundaries, guiding user attention effectively.
- Implied Depth through Perspective: Use one-point or two-point perspective to create a strong sense of spatial recession, even in two-dimensional representations, enhancing the perception of depth and volume.
