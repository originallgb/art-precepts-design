## Precept Critique
This artifact demonstrates the efficacy of combining precise linear definition with broad color washes to delineate distinct geographical entities and their interrelationships. The integration of iconic representations for specific landmarks within a functional cartographic system provides immediate contextual anchoring. Its hand-rendered quality underscores the value of deliberate, human-scale information encoding, offering a counterpoint to purely algorithmic data visualization.
