## Precept Critique
This work systematically isolates and presents the perceptual dynamics of color interaction and planar organization within a constrained rectilinear framework. It functions as an empirical study, demonstrating how precise chromatic and spatial relationships generate distinct visual weight and hierarchical tension without relying on representational content. The deliberate material choices and minimalist composition underscore the Bauhaus pedagogical emphasis on foundational elements as primary drivers of aesthetic and functional communication.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is drawn sequentially between the upper magenta rectangle and the lower green rectangle, mediated by the neutral grey field. The vertical stacking creates a primary top-to-bottom reading, with each colored form acting as a distinct visual anchor.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is founded on a strict rectilinear grid. Two precisely cut rectangular forms are positioned within a larger rectangular canvas, exhibiting parallel alignment along their horizontal axes and a clear vertical separation, creating a balanced yet dynamic planar arrangement.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The composition exhibits an asymmetrical balance. While the two colored rectangles are of identical dimensions and are centrally aligned on the vertical axis, their placement is offset from the canvas's geometric center, creating a subtle tension that is resolved by the equal visual weight of the forms against the neutral background.
