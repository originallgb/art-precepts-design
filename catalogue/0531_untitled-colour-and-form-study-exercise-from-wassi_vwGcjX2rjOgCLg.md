## Precept Critique
This study rigorously isolates and demonstrates the perceptual impact of chromatic contrast and value shifts within identical modular grid structures. It functions as a foundational exercise in visual system design, illustrating how fundamental elements generate distinct visual experiences. The work underscores the Bauhaus principle of systematic inquiry into form and color relationships, providing a blueprint for structured visual communication.

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is drawn sequentially to the two distinct rectangular grid systems, first the high-contrast checkerboard, then the lower-contrast yellow and brown grid, with no singular focal point within each system due to uniform distribution.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is fundamentally based on two orthogonal grid structures, each defining a rectangular field. The top grid employs a 6x4 matrix, while the bottom utilizes a 7x4 matrix, both contained within faint pencil guidelines.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Both grid systems exhibit strong bilateral symmetry along their central axes, with visual weight evenly distributed across their respective fields. The overall composition maintains a balanced, static equilibrium between the two distinct patterns.
