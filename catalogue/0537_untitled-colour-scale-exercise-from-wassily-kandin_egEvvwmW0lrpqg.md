## Precept Critique
This piece rigorously demonstrates the power of fundamental geometric forms and primary colors to establish visual hierarchy and direct attention. It serves as a didactic example of how precise compositional choices, even with minimal elements, can systematically explore optical phenomena. The work's strength lies in its reductionist approach, isolating and examining the core mechanics of visual perception and design principles.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Utilize concentric forms to create an undeniable focal point, drawing user attention to a central information node or action.

- Introduce subtle asymmetry to a geometrically balanced core to prevent static compositions and inject dynamic visual interest without sacrificing clarity.
- Systematically explore the impact of primary color relationships on perceived depth and visual weight within a constrained palette.
- Leverage the inherent honesty of material and construction (e.g., collage edges) to add tactile authenticity and subtle textural variation to digital or physical interfaces.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The concentric circles create a strong centripetal flow, directing the viewer's gaze inward towards the black core. The offset placement of the target and the two white dots on the left introduce a subtle leftward pull, counteracting perfect centrality.
- **Geometric Structure**: The primary geometric structure is a series of concentric circles, forming a target motif. This central element is positioned off-center to the right, against a rectangular background. Two small, isolated circular elements are placed vertically on the left margin.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The composition exhibits asymmetrical balance. While the target itself is radially symmetrical, its placement off-center and the presence of the two small white dots on the opposing side disrupt overall bilateral symmetry, creating a dynamic equilibrium.
