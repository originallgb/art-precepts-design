## Precept Critique
This piece rigorously demonstrates the foundational principles of linear perspective, providing a blueprint for constructing spatial depth on a two-dimensional plane. Its explicit rendering of orthogonal lines and vanishing points offers a clear instructional model for visual architects designing immersive digital environments or physical spaces. The precise application of geometric rules to define form and shadow illustrates how systematic visual logic can create compelling, navigable spatial experiences.

- For complex interfaces or architectural plans, render underlying geometric grids or perspective lines to clarify spatial relationships and guide user understanding of depth.
