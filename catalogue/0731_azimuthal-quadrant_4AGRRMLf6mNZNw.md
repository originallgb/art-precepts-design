## Precept Critique
This instrument exemplifies the principle of form following function, where every structural element directly contributes to its precise measurement capabilities. Its modular construction, combining robust wood for stability with finely calibrated brass for accuracy, demonstrates an optimized material hierarchy. The clear articulation of its mechanical components provides an intuitive understanding of its operational logic, a hallmark of effective functional design.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Hierarchical Materiality: Employ distinct materials with properties optimized for specific functional roles (e.g., heavy wood for stability, precise brass for measurement).
- Exposed Mechanism as Instruction: Design components to be visible and understandable, allowing users to infer function from form and construction.
- Structural Clarity: Utilize orthogonal and radial bracing to convey stability and precision, reinforcing the instrument's purpose through its visual grammar.
- Modular Assembly for Adaptability: Design systems with clearly defined, separable components that can be adjusted or replaced, enhancing longevity and serviceability.
