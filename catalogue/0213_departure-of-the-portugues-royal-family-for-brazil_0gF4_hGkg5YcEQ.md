## Precept Critique
This composition masterfully employs a dominant diagonal element to direct attention, anchoring a complex narrative of human activity against an expansive, atmospheric backdrop. The strategic distribution of visual density, contrasting a bustling foreground with a serene sky, effectively communicates the scale and gravity of a historical event. Its use of diffuse light and a mixed chromatic palette creates a nuanced emotional landscape, balancing the urgency of departure with the solemnity of the occasion.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is drawn from the prominent diagonal mast on the left, down to the dense crowd and boats in the lower left, then sweeps right along the shoreline, following the procession of carriages and figures towards the city in the mid-ground right. The expansive sky provides a counter-balance to the busy lower half.
- **Geometric Structure**: A strong diagonal axis is established by the large mast, intersecting with the horizontal plane of the water and shoreline. The composition is structured around a lower band of dense human activity and a vast, open upper sky, creating a two-thirds sky, one-third land/water division.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance. The visual weight of the large mast and clustered figures on the left is counteracted by the extended procession of carriages and buildings on the right, with the expansive sky providing atmospheric equilibrium.
