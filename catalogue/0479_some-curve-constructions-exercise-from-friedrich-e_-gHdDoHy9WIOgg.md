## Precept Critique
This piece exemplifies the didactic power of visual decomposition, rendering complex mathematical principles into comprehensible, sequential steps through precise graphic articulation. Its systematic application of color-coding and line weight differentiates functional layers within a single diagram, establishing a clear visual hierarchy for instructional efficacy. The rigorous adherence to geometric primitives and grid-based organization demonstrates how foundational structural rules can generate diverse forms while maintaining visual coherence and analytical clarity.
