## Precept Critique
This artifact exemplifies the systematic decomposition of architectural and product design into discrete, measurable components, facilitating precise communication and fabrication. Its reliance on orthogonal and isometric projections demonstrates a foundational methodology for translating three-dimensional concepts into two-dimensional, actionable instructions. The clear hierarchy of information, from overall spatial layout to individual furniture details, establishes a scalable framework for complex system design.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ orthogonal projection for clear, unambiguous representation of spatial relationships and object dimensions in technical documentation.
- Utilize isometric views to provide intuitive three-dimensional context for individual components within a larger system, enhancing comprehension.
- Establish a consistent typographic hierarchy for annotations and labels to guide information consumption and denote scale or functional purpose.
- Design modular components that can be arrayed and reconfigured to optimize spatial utility and manufacturing efficiency.
- Integrate detailed component views with overall spatial plans to ensure holistic system understanding and prevent design inconsistencies.
