## Precept Critique
This composition rigorously explores the dynamic interplay between positive and negative space through a non-hierarchical rectilinear grid, challenging conventional notions of figure-ground relationships. It demonstrates how precise chromatic contrast and irregular modularity can generate visual rhythm and direct attention without relying on traditional focal points or explicit directional cues. The work provides a foundational study in spatial partitioning and the creation of perceived depth through planar juxtaposition, offering insights into interface segmentation and architectural massing.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye navigates a non-linear, distributed path, guided by the interconnected white linear elements that delineate the red planar forms. There is no singular focal point; instead, attention is continuously redirected across the entire surface through a dynamic, interlocking network.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is built upon a rectilinear, modular grid system, intentionally disrupted by varied rectangle dimensions and non-uniform alignment, creating an irregular yet structured spatial partitioning.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The composition exhibits asymmetrical balance, where visual weight is distributed through the varied sizes and placements of the red rectangles and the consistent width of the white interstitial lines, preventing any single area from dominating.
