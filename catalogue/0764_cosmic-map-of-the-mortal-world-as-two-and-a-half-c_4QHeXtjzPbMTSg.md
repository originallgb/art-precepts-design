## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Hierarchical Information Layering: Organize complex data into concentric or radial layers, where each layer represents a distinct conceptual level or scope, allowing users to progressively delve into detail.
- Pattern-Based Segmentation: Utilize distinct visual patterns and color palettes for different segments or zones within a structured layout to enhance differentiation and reduce cognitive load during navigation.
- Integrated Textual and Iconic Cues: Combine concise textual labels with recognizable iconic representations directly within the visual field to provide redundant coding for critical information, improving comprehension and recall.
- Centripetal-Centrifugal Navigation: Design interfaces or spatial layouts that encourage a natural flow from a central, high-level overview outwards to detailed peripheral information, or vice-versa, based on user intent.
- Formal Symmetry for Stability: Employ radial or bilateral symmetry in foundational layouts to convey stability, order, and a sense of completeness, particularly for systems representing universal or foundational concepts.
