## Precept Critique
This work systematically dissects fundamental geometric relationships and color interactions within a constrained formal system, serving as a didactic exploration of visual principles. It demonstrates how simple, repeatable elements can generate complex visual structures and explore principles of balance and tension through precise compositional rules. The piece functions as a foundational study, illustrating the systematic organization of form and modular design applicable across various visual disciplines.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Modular Decomposition: Break down complex visual problems into fundamental geometric units (squares, triangles) to understand their interrelationships and potential for recombination.
- Grid-Based Structuring: Utilize underlying grids to establish order, proportion, and alignment, even when introducing dynamic or diagonal elements, ensuring visual coherence.
- Color as a Structural Element: Employ distinct color blocks to define planes, emphasize boundaries, and create visual hierarchy within a geometric composition, rather than merely decorative application.
- Transparency for Layering: Implement semi-transparent elements to reveal underlying structures and create shallow depth without disrupting the planar quality of the design, enhancing informational density.
- Formal Constraint as Creative Catalyst: Define strict formal boundaries (e.g., 'Form in the format of a square') to drive innovative solutions and systematic exploration within a predefined framework.
