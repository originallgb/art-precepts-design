## Precept Critique
This piece systematically deconstructs color relationships through precise geometric partitioning, establishing a foundational visual grammar for chromatic interaction. Its objective presentation of color theory demonstrates how abstract principles can be rendered with clarity and formal rigor, serving as a didactic tool for visual analysis. The work's emphasis on modularity and elemental forms provides a blueprint for constructing complex visual systems from discrete, well-defined components.
