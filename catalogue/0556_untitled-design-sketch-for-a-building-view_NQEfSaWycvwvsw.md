## Precept Critique
This sketch demonstrates the power of reductive geometry to convey complex architectural massing and spatial relationships with minimal visual information. It highlights how fundamental volumetric primitives, when axially aligned and proportionally balanced, can establish both monumental presence and functional clarity. The work serves as a foundational study in abstracting structural intent, offering a blueprint for interface or product design where form follows an essentialized functional logic.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Prioritize axial symmetry and verticality to establish immediate visual stability and hierarchical dominance in complex structures.
- Employ volumetric primitives (cylinders, rectangles) to define distinct functional zones within a unified composition.
- Utilize subtle shading and line weight variation to convey depth and material presence without relying on complex textures or color.
- Design for modularity, where distinct components (cylinders, central spine, base) can be understood independently yet contribute to a cohesive whole.
- Leverage negative space around a central form to amplify its perceived monumentality and focus user attention.
