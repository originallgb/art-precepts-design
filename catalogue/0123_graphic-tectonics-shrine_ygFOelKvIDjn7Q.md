## Precept Critique
This piece demonstrates how systematic variation of a simple geometric primitive can generate complex spatial perception without relying on traditional perspective. The precise interplay of line weight and overlap establishes a hierarchical reading of planes, guiding the eye through an ambiguous, yet logically constructed, volumetric space. Its power lies in revealing the inherent dynamism within static, orthogonal forms, offering a foundational lesson in visual rhythm and structural integrity for any design system.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ systematic variation of line weight and density to articulate depth and hierarchy within planar compositions.
- Utilize overlapping rectilinear elements to create perceptual ambiguity and dynamic spatial relationships, challenging static interpretations.
- Design modular systems where simple, repeatable units generate complex, emergent patterns through their interaction and nesting.
- Leverage monochrome palettes to emphasize structural form and optical effects, reducing chromatic distraction.
- Implement precise orthogonal grids as foundational frameworks for generating intricate, yet ordered, visual structures.
