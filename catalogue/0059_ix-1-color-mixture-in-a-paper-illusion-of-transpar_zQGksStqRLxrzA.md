## Precept Critique
This composition rigorously demonstrates the perceptual phenomenon of subtractive color mixing and illusory transparency through precise geometric superimposition. It systematically isolates variables of hue, value, and saturation to reveal how perceived color shifts based on adjacent and underlying planes. The work functions as a foundational visual experiment, offering direct insights into optical interaction crucial for interface design and spatial color application.
