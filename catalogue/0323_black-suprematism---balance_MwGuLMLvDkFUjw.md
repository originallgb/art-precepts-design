## Precept Critique
This composition exemplifies the power of reductive geometry to establish dynamic equilibrium and spatial tension with minimal elements. The deliberate diagonal placement of two distinct masses against an expansive void demonstrates how asymmetry can generate visual interest and direct focal flow more effectively than static symmetry. Its foundational exploration of form and negative space provides a blueprint for interface design, architectural massing, and product aesthetics where clarity and impact are paramount.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Utilize off-axis or unevenly weighted elements to create visual tension and guide user attention, rather than relying solely on static, centered layouts.
- Ensure absolute distinction between primary content (figure) and background (ground) through high contrast and minimal visual noise, enhancing immediate comprehension.
- Treat negative space not as emptiness, but as an active component that defines and amplifies the presence of positive forms, improving readability and perceived spaciousness.
- Employ variations in size and proximity between geometric elements to establish clear visual hierarchy and suggest relationships or progression within a system.
