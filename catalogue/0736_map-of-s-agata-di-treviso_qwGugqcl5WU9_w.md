## Precept Critique
This artifact demonstrates the efficacy of a limited color palette and clear linear demarcation for conveying complex spatial information with high legibility. Its reliance on explicit textual annotation integrated directly into the visual field establishes a robust information hierarchy without requiring external legend lookups. The deliberate use of negative space around key elements ensures visual separation and reduces cognitive load, a critical consideration for data-dense interfaces.
