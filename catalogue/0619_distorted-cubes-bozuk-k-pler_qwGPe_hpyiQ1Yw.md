## Precept Critique
This work demonstrates the power of systematic variation within a rigid framework, illustrating how permutations of simple geometric forms and a controlled color palette can generate complex visual interest. It underscores the principle that perceived three-dimensionality can be achieved through color differentiation and isometric projection, rather than traditional linear perspective. The composition exemplifies modularity and the aesthetic impact of combinatorial design, providing a blueprint for structured visual information architecture.
