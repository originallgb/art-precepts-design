## Precept Critique

The integration of rectilinear and curvilinear forms, alongside a restrained material palette, establishes a precedent for elegant, utilitarian design.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Implement visible, functional hardware as a design feature to communicate structural integrity and operational mechanisms.

- Utilize a limited, complementary material palette to achieve visual coherence and emphasize material honesty.
- Integrate subtle geometric variations (e.g., rectilinear base with curvilinear top) to introduce visual interest without compromising functional clarity.
- Employ slatted or open frameworks in lower sections to reduce perceived visual mass and maintain a sense of lightness.
