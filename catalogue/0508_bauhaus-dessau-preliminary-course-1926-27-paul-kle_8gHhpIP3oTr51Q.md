## Precept Critique
This work rigorously investigates the combinatorial possibilities of orthogonal subdivision within a fixed boundary, demonstrating how complex visual rhythms and hierarchies emerge from elementary geometric operations. It serves as a foundational study in modularity and spatial partitioning, illustrating principles of visual weight distribution and focal direction through varying line densities. The systematic exploration of horizontal versus vertical dominance within the two compositions provides a clear comparative analysis of structural emphasis, directly informing interface layout and architectural planning.
