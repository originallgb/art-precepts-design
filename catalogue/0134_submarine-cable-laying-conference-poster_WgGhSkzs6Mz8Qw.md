## Precept Critique
This artifact demonstrates effective information hierarchy through scale and placement, guiding the viewer from the subject to the logistical details. The integration of illustrative metaphor with textual content provides immediate conceptual understanding, enhancing memorability and engagement for a technical topic. Its raw, hand-rendered aesthetic communicates authenticity and directness, establishing a distinct visual voice for functional communication.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is initially drawn to the central title 'CABLES SOUS-MARINS', then horizontally across the illustrative elements of the boat and buoy, before descending through the event details. A strong horizontal axis is established by the waterline and reinforced by the text blocks.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is structured around a dominant horizontal division created by the waterline, separating the upper informational text from the lower logistical details. Vertical elements like the boat and buoy provide counterpoints, while the main title forms a robust rectangular block.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance is achieved through the visual weight of the boat on the left and the larger, more saturated buoy on the right, with the prominent central text block anchoring the upper half. The overall distribution is stable despite the lack of strict symmetry.
