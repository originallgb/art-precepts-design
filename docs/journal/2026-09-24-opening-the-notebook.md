# 24 September 2026: opening the notebook

I want more of my work to be visible. Looking for my next role has made
that more pressing, but this project started with something much more
personal: years of saved art, and a wish to do something with it.

Getting it ready to share has become an experiment of its own. I've been
recovering scripts, retracing decisions and trying to explain the project
without making the introduction as long as the project itself.

There is plenty left to do. The catalogue exists; the design playbooks
don't. The clustering is provisional. The original app makes the intended
experience tangible, but it isn't connected to the later pipeline.

I don't want finishing everything to become the condition for showing
anything. The first public version can be a useful working record, with
its limits stated clearly. Protecting private information and respecting
source material matter before publication. A more polished interface,
more analysis and a complete set of playbooks can follow.

The next experiment is small: take one generated observation, write down
what I make of it, and try it in a design. Keep the original suggestion
beside the result so it's possible to see what changed and why.

This entry records the decision to share the work in progress. Publication
and permission checks are tracked separately in the
[release checklist](../process/publication-checklist.md).
