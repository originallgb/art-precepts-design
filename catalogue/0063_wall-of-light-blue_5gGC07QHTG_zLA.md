## Precept Critique
This composition demonstrates the power of modularity in generating complex visual rhythm from simple rectilinear units, enabling scalable design systems. The strategic juxtaposition of chromatic temperatures and value contrasts within a constrained palette creates an internal luminosity, directing attention without explicit focal points. Its shallow, interlocking spatial organization offers a model for constructing layered interfaces or architectural facades that balance material presence with implied depth.
