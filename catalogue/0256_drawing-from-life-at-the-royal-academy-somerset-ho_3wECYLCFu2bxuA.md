## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The primary focal point is the nude model, illuminated by the overhead lamp. The viewer's eye then tracks along the tiered rows of students, each engaged in drawing, creating a radial flow that reinforces the central subject. Secondary attention is drawn to the individual drawing boards and their localized light sources.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is structured around a central, implied vertical axis anchored by the model and overhead lamp. A strong horizontal baseline is established by the students' benches, which curve into a semi-circular, tiered arrangement. The room itself forms a rectangular enclosure, providing a stable frame for the dynamic internal elements.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The composition exhibits asymmetrical balance. The central model is slightly offset to the left, counterbalanced by the greater visual mass and activity of the students extending further to the right. The large overhead lamp, while centrally positioned, directs its light towards the left, contributing to this dynamic equilibrium.
