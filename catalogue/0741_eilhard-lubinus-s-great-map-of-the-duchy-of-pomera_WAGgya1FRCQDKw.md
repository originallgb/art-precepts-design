## Precept Critique
This artifact exemplifies the strategic integration of disparate data streams—geographical, genealogical, and urban—into a cohesive, hierarchically organized visual system. Its meticulous framing and annotation zones demonstrate how peripheral information can contextualize and enrich a primary data display without causing visual interference. The consistent application of a formal visual language across diverse elements establishes a robust model for communicating authority and comprehensive knowledge through design.
