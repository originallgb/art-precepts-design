## Precept Critique
This artifact exemplifies the iterative nature of design ideation, where foundational geometric principles are explored through precise line work and overlaid conceptual sketches. The composition demonstrates how visual hierarchy can be established through line weight and detail density, directing attention from a resolved primary concept to adjacent, less defined explorations. The deliberate use of a muted substrate and subtle graphite underscores a focus on form and structure, prioritizing functional clarity over chromatic or textural embellishment.
