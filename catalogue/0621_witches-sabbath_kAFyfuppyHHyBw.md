## Precept Critique
The deliberate off-center placement of the dominant figure, coupled with the radial arrangement of supplicants, establishes a clear hierarchical focus and directs viewer attention through a defined visual pathway. Goya employs a stark chiaroscuro to delineate form and emotional intensity, leveraging extreme tonal contrast to isolate key narrative elements against an oppressive, undifferentiated background. The integration of grotesque symbolism within a naturalistic setting creates a potent semiotic friction, challenging conventional interpretations of power dynamics and societal anxieties.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The primary focal point is the central goat figure, around which the supplicant figures are arranged in a semi-circular arc, directing the eye inward towards the goat's gesture and the infants. Secondary attention is drawn to the moon and bats in the upper left, providing an atmospheric anchor.
- **Geometric Structure**: A strong central vertical axis is established by the goat. The surrounding figures form an inverted U-shape or semi-circle, creating a contained, ritualistic space. The landscape provides a subtle diagonal recession from left to right.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance, with the dominant goat figure and the cluster of figures on the right counterbalanced by the more sparse arrangement on the left and the expansive, dark sky. Visual weight is concentrated in the lower two-thirds of the canvas.
- **Framing Density**: dense
- **Historical Lineage**: Romanticism (Goya's 'Black Paintings'), Spanish Baroque (dramatic lighting, intense emotion), Grotesque art

### 2. Utility & Ergonomics
- **Structural Motifs**: Centralized dominant figure, Circular/semi-circular arrangement of secondary figures, Dramatic diagonal recession in landscape, High contrast for emotional impact
- **Affordance Takeaway**: High utility for framing, content pacing, and visual hierarchy.

### 3. Visual Language & Semiotics
- **Core Symbols**: Goat (Devil/Satan), Moon, Bats, Infants/Skeletons (Sacrifice/Death), Coven/Witches, Laurel Wreath (Mockery of Authority)
- **Emotional Valence**: Foreboding, dread, ritualistic horror, despair, submission, grotesque.
- **Narrative Themes**: Superstition, Religious fanaticism, Sacrifice, Corruption of innocence, Social critique, The macabre

### 4. Light, Space & Materiality
- **Light Profile**: chiaroscuro
