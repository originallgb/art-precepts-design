## Precept Critique
This composition rigorously demonstrates how precise geometric nesting and controlled chromatic shifts generate perceptual depth and focal hierarchy without relying on traditional perspective. The deliberate use of a single, thin white line to define an internal boundary within a larger color field illustrates a powerful mechanism for visual separation and emphasis. It serves as a foundational study in how minimal elements, when systematically arranged, can produce complex optical phenomena and direct user attention.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ concentric framing to establish clear focal points and guide user attention inward through progressive layers of information.
- Utilize subtle color shifts within a consistent geometric structure to imply depth and hierarchy without introducing complex shading or perspective.
- Introduce high-contrast, thin linear elements to delineate boundaries or highlight specific regions within otherwise uniform color fields.
- Design for absolute symmetry in core structural elements to convey stability, balance, and a sense of deliberate order.
