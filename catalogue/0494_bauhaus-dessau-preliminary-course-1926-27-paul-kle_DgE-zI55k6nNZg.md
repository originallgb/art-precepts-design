## Precept Critique
This work systematically dissects the interplay of positive and negative space within a modular framework, demonstrating how spatial relationships dictate visual weight and hierarchy. It provides a foundational comparative study of achromatic void manipulation against chromatic form articulation, illustrating distinct approaches to compositional balance. The piece functions as a didactic artifact, codifying principles of geometric abstraction and color theory into an accessible, actionable visual grammar for design students.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Modular Comparison Principle: Present contrasting design solutions side-by-side within a consistent framework to facilitate direct comparison and highlight specific design variables (e.g., positive vs. negative space, color palettes).
- Figure-Ground Reversibility for UI: Design interface elements where the 'active' state can be defined by either filled shapes (positive) or defined voids (negative), allowing for flexible visual hierarchy and interaction states.
- Primary Color as Functional Signifier: Utilize a limited palette of primary colors (red, yellow, black) against a neutral background to create immediate visual distinction and assign clear functional or categorical meaning to elements.
- Orthogonal Grid for Content Organization: Employ a strict orthogonal grid to arrange content blocks, ensuring visual stability, predictable alignment, and efficient use of space, even with asymmetrical internal arrangements.
- Negative Space as Active Design Element: Treat negative space not as empty filler, but as a deliberate, shaped component that actively defines and frames positive elements, guiding user attention and creating visual rhythm.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The viewer's eye is drawn horizontally between the two distinct square modules, then internally within each module. The left module's negative spaces create an 'E'-like path, guiding the eye around the central grey mass. The right module's colored rectangles direct attention from the vertical red block, across to the horizontal yellow, and down to the horizontal black, establishing a clear, sequential read.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is anchored by two equidistant, self-contained square modules. Within these modules, orthogonal rectangular forms are precisely arranged, defining either positive colored shapes or negative void spaces. This establishes a rigorous grid-based, modular framework.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The overall composition achieves horizontal balance through the equal visual weight and spacing of the two square modules. Internally, each module exhibits an asymmetrical balance of rectangular forms, where varied sizes and positions maintain equilibrium without mirroring.
