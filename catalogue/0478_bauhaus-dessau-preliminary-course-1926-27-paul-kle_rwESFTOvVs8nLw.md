## Precept Critique
This piece demonstrates the power of recursive geometric subdivision to establish a clear visual hierarchy and focal point without relying on color or shading. Its rigorous adherence to a simple rule set generates complex visual information, illustrating how foundational principles can scale to create intricate systems. The work serves as a didactic example of spatial organization, where diminishing scale effectively guides attention and implies infinite depth within a two-dimensional plane.
