## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is initially drawn to the central, elevated escutcheon, then descends along the decorative garlands and architectural framing of the central niche. Secondary attention is directed to the right-hand panel, which provides contextual architectural information, before returning to the base measurements.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is anchored by a strong vertical axis through the central altar, flanked by implied pilasters and a rectilinear base. Nested rectangular and arched forms define the primary architectural elements, with a broken pediment structure at the apex. The overall structure is a modular assembly of planar surfaces and volumetric projections.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The central altar design exhibits bilateral symmetry, emphasizing its focal importance. However, the inclusion of the adjacent 'Fianco' (side) elevation on the right introduces a deliberate functional asymmetry, balancing the detailed central element with a broader contextual view of the surrounding architectural space.
