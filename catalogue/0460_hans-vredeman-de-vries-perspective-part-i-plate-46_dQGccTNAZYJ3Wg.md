## Precept Critique
This work demonstrates the power of rigorous linear perspective to construct an illusion of infinite depth and architectural grandeur within a two-dimensional plane. The systematic application of geometric principles creates a highly structured, navigable visual space, even in a fantastical context. It serves as a foundational example of how precise spatial rendering can direct user attention and establish a sense of order and scale.
