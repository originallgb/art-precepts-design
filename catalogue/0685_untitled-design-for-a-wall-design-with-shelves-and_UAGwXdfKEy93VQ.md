## Precept Critique
This sketch exemplifies the foundational principle of modularity in spatial design, demonstrating how a rectilinear grid can establish both structural integrity and flexible organizational capacity. Its low-fidelity execution prioritizes conceptual clarity and functional arrangement over aesthetic rendering, highlighting the iterative nature of design ideation. The deliberate asymmetry within a balanced framework illustrates a nuanced approach to visual interest within a highly ordered system.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ a consistent rectilinear grid to define primary spatial divisions, allowing for flexible arrangement and rearrangement of internal components without compromising overall structural coherence.
- Prioritize quick, gestural sketching over detailed rendering in early design phases to facilitate rapid exploration of multiple configurations and functional relationships.
- Introduce subtle asymmetries in sub-elements or content placement within a larger, balanced framework to maintain visual interest and prevent static monotony.
- Use minimal line work and implied forms to suggest functionality (e.g., shelves, display areas), relying on the user's cognitive completion to understand potential interactions or storage.
- Direct user attention by varying the density of lines and the level of detail within different sections of a layout, drawing the eye to areas of higher functional or informational importance.
