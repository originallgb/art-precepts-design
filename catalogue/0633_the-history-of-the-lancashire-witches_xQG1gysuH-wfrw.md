## Precept Critique
This artifact demonstrates how constrained media can enforce robust information hierarchy through stark contrast and deliberate spatial partitioning. The integration of illustrative content within a rigid textual grid establishes a clear visual rhythm, guiding reader engagement without relying on complex graphical elements. Its enduring legibility and narrative coherence underscore the power of fundamental design principles over elaborate aesthetic embellishments.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Utilize stark foreground-background contrast (e.g., dark text on light background) to ensure immediate legibility and differentiate primary content from substrate.
- Employ consistent, simple frames (e.g., rectangular borders) to encapsulate distinct content modules (illustrations, text blocks), providing visual separation and structural clarity.
- Establish clear information hierarchy by varying font size and weight for headings, subheadings, and body text, even within a limited typeface palette.
- Implement simple, repeatable decorative elements (e.g., repeating glyphs or patterns) as functional dividers to break up long text passages and signal content transitions without introducing visual noise.
- Allow the inherent qualities of the medium (e.g., paper texture, ink impression) to contribute to the overall aesthetic and communicate authenticity or historical context.
