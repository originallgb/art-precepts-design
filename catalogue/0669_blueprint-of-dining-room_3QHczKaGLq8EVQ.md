## Precept Critique
This blueprint exemplifies the power of modularity and hierarchical organization in communicating complex spatial information with clarity. Its precise line work and consistent visual language establish a robust system for defining architectural elements and their relationships. The deliberate contrast between technical drawing and calligraphic annotation effectively guides interpretation and adds a layer of human craft to objective data.
