## Precept Critique
This composition exemplifies the strategic use of linear perspective and layered depth to guide visual progression through a complex spatial arrangement. The interplay between rigid architectural geometry and organic botanical forms establishes a dynamic visual tension that prevents static interpretation. It demonstrates how controlled contrast in texture and color can delineate distinct zones within a unified design, enhancing both legibility and aesthetic richness.
