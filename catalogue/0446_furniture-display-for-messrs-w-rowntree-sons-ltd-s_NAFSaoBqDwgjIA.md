## Precept Critique
This work exemplifies systematic visual communication for complex product systems, leveraging orthogonal projection and numbered annotations to convey functional relationships and spatial arrangements. The consistent application of a geometric grid and controlled color palette establishes a clear visual hierarchy, directing attention to key design elements and their proposed integration within a domestic environment. Its utility lies in demonstrating how abstract design principles—modularity, spatial efficiency, and user-centric arrangement—can be concretely articulated through precise technical illustration for manufacturing and consumer understanding.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ orthogonal grids and linear perspective to clearly define spatial relationships and object placement in complex systems.
- Utilize a limited, high-contrast color palette (e.g., primary accent on neutral base) to highlight critical components or user interaction points.
- Implement numbered annotations and corresponding textual descriptions to provide explicit functional details for modular components.
- Design modular units with consistent visual language to communicate system scalability and reconfigurability.
- Use a clear, functional typographic style for all labels and instructional text to maintain analytical clarity.
