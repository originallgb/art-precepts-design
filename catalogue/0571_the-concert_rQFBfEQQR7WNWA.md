### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is initially drawn to the standing figure on the right, then guided diagonally leftward across the seated figures and the harpsichord, before settling on the patterned rug and the foreground instrument. The strong orthogonal lines of the floor tiles and the horizontal alignment of the paintings provide a secondary, stabilizing visual path.
- **Geometric Structure**: A robust diagonal grid is established by the checkerboard floor, creating a deep recession into the pictorial space. This is counterbalanced by the rectilinear framework of the wall and the two prominent framed paintings, which anchor the upper half of the composition. Figures and furniture are arranged within a subtle triangular formation, with the standing woman and the harpsichord forming the apex and base respectively.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The composition exhibits asymmetrical balance, with the visual weight distributed by the grouping of three figures and the large harpsichord on the right, counteracted by the substantial draped table and foreground instrument on the left. The negative space of the wall provides a necessary visual respite, preventing overcrowding.
