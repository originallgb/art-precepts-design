## Precept Critique
This work demonstrates the potent communicative power of extreme contrast and simplified form, efficiently conveying complex cultural narratives through minimal visual information. The deliberate use of negative space to define skeletal features exemplifies how absence can be as informative as presence in visual design. Its enduring cultural resonance underscores the efficacy of archetypal imagery in establishing immediate and profound symbolic connections.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The viewer's gaze is immediately drawn to the high-contrast skeletal faces, then follows the figures' outlines and the diagonal hatching patterns, moving from left to right across the pair.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is anchored by two strong vertical forms representing the figures, with an implied central axis between them. Diagonal hatching within the forms provides textural depth and breaks the rigidity of the silhouettes.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance is achieved through the slightly offset positioning and differing postures of the two figures, with the female figure's more dynamic pose counteracting the male's static stance, distributing visual weight effectively.
