## Precept Critique
This artifact demonstrates how high-contrast color blocking and simplified geometric forms effectively communicate object identity and affordance for interaction. Its modular construction, where distinct components form a cohesive whole, provides an intuitive framework for guided assembly and problem-solving. The precise use of negative space and defining outlines clarifies boundaries, reducing cognitive load and enhancing the user's ability to discern individual elements within the system.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Utilize high-contrast color palettes to delineate distinct interactive components, enhancing their visibility and perceived affordance.
- Design modular systems where each component possesses a unique, interlocking geometry, inherently guiding users through assembly processes.
- Employ negative space as a functional element to precisely define the boundaries and relationships between positive forms, simplifying spatial understanding.
- Reduce complex visual information to essential geometric primitives and primary colors for universal recognition and reduced cognitive processing.
- Integrate subtle material cues (e.g., simulated wood grain, worn edges) to convey authenticity and a sense of tangible interaction, even in digital contexts.
