## Precept Critique
This composition demonstrates the efficacy of modular geometric primitives in establishing complex spatial relationships through layering and juxtaposition, rather than linear perspective. The calculated interplay of opaque color fields and varied scales generates a dynamic visual hierarchy, directing attention without explicit directional cues. It exemplifies how formal abstraction can communicate structural integrity and controlled energy, offering a blueprint for interface design that prioritizes clarity and functional separation.
