### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is immediately drawn to the central, semi-nude figure of the Rake undergoing treatment, then moves outwards to the surrounding figures, following their gazes and gestures. A secondary flow is established by the diagonal of the staircase on the right, leading upwards to other patients.
- **Geometric Structure**: The scene is contained within a rectilinear room, defined by walls, doors, and a barred window. This architectural framework provides a stable, grid-like foundation against which the chaotic arrangement of figures is contrasted. Implied diagonals are formed by the Rake's body and the various limbs and instruments, creating dynamic tension.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The composition is asymmetrical. The left and central areas are heavily weighted with the main action and a dense cluster of figures. The right side, while less dense, is balanced by the verticality of the staircase and the figures positioned upon it, preventing a complete collapse of visual weight to the left.
