## Precept Critique
This piece systematically deconstructs chromatic relationships through a controlled, modular framework, demonstrating how sequential color shifts can establish visual hierarchy and emotional trajectory. Its pedagogical origin underscores the power of elemental visual components to convey complex theoretical principles without relying on representational imagery. The deliberate material application, revealing pigment and substrate, emphasizes process and material honesty as integral to the aesthetic and conceptual outcome.
