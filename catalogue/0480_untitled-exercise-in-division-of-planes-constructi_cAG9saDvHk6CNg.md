## Precept Critique
This piece rigorously demonstrates the systematic decomposition of fundamental geometric forms, illustrating how complex structures can be derived from simple elements through precise proportional relationships. It functions as a foundational exercise in visual grammar, emphasizing the analytical construction of form rather than intuitive expression. The controlled use of color and line weight establishes a clear hierarchy of information, making the underlying structural logic immediately legible.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is drawn to the central intersection of lines within each geometric construction, then outward along the radiating and intersecting lines. The two constructions are vertically stacked, creating a top-down reading order.
- **Geometric Structure**: Each construction is based on a square and a circle, with internal divisions forming triangles and rectangles. The top figure features a square containing a circle, while the bottom features a square rotated 45 degrees (diamond) containing a circle, which in turn contains an axis-aligned square. Both demonstrate a rigorous, modular, and orthogonal/diagonal grid system.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Both geometric constructions exhibit perfect radial and axial symmetry. The overall composition maintains vertical balance, presenting two equally weighted studies separated by negative space.
