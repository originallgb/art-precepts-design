## Precept Critique
This work demonstrates the power of serial variation within a constrained formal system to generate visual interest and rhythmic complexity. It meticulously explores the interplay between positive form and negative space, where the void actively shapes the perception of the solid. The composition's strength lies in its disciplined repetition, which paradoxically highlights the subtle, incremental deviations of each module.
