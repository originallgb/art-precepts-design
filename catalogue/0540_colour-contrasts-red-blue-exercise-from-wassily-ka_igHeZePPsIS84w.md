## Precept Critique
This piece rigorously demonstrates the perceptual dynamics of chromatic opposition and geometric containment, isolating the interaction between two highly saturated hues within a stable, symmetrical framework. It serves as a foundational study in how precise formal relationships can generate visual tension and equilibrium without representational content. The work's power lies in its reductionist approach, forcing an examination of elemental visual forces applicable to interface design or architectural massing.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Centralized Focus through Chromatic Contrast: Employ high-contrast color pairings for primary interactive elements to ensure immediate user attention within a stable, low-contrast background.
- Geometric Containment for Information Hierarchy: Utilize nested or concentric geometric forms to establish clear hierarchical relationships, guiding the eye from general context to specific detail.
- Static Equilibrium for System Stability: Design interfaces or spatial layouts with perfect or near-perfect symmetry and balanced visual weight to convey reliability and predictability.
- Material Honesty in Digital Textures: Render digital surfaces with subtle, consistent texture to imply tangibility and depth, avoiding overly glossy or flat representations unless intentionally abstract.
- Limited Palette for Conceptual Clarity: Restrict color palettes to a few highly impactful, contrasting hues to emphasize core functions or conceptual distinctions, reducing cognitive load.
