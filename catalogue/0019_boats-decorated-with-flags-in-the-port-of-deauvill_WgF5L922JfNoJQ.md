## Precept Critique
This composition demonstrates the efficacy of high-frequency detail clusters against expansive, lower-frequency fields to manage visual weight and direct attention. The dynamic interplay of vertical elements against horizontal strata establishes a robust yet fluid structural framework. Its success lies in balancing atmospheric rendering with precise, localized chromatic accents, creating an active visual experience without relying on deep spatial recession.
