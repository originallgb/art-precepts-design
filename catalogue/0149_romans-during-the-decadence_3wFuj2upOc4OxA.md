## Precept Critique
This composition masterfully employs classical architectural frameworks to contain and contrast human chaos, demonstrating how rigid structure can amplify narrative tension. The strategic placement of static, idealized forms against dynamic, flawed figures establishes a clear moral commentary without explicit didacticism. Its sophisticated use of light and shadow defines spatial depth and emotional weight, directing the viewer's interpretation through visual emphasis rather than overt symbolism.
