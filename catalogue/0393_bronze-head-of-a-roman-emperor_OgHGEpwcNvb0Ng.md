## Precept Critique
The deliberate use of high-contrast lighting against an absolute void isolates the subject, forcing an analytical engagement with its volumetric form and surface degradation. The artifact's physical damage and material patina function as embedded historical data, communicating temporal passage and past events more effectively than pristine preservation. The absence of inlaid eyes transforms a representational portrait into an unsettling, universal symbol of lost identity or detached observation, leveraging negative space for psychological impact.
