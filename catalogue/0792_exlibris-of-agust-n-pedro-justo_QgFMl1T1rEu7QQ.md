## Precept Critique
This piece effectively leverages a rigid hierarchical structure, employing nested frames and distinct visual zones to segment information and guide interpretive flow. The duotone palette and high-contrast linework establish immediate legibility, while intricate allegorical elements encode complex narratives within a constrained visual footprint. Its enduring utility lies in demonstrating how symbolic density and formal composition can convey institutional authority and personal values through a compact, reproducible graphic.
