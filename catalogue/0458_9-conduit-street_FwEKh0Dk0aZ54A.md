## Precept Critique
This architectural rendering exemplifies the power of orthogonal projection to convey structural clarity and proportional relationships, essential for communicating complex spatial designs. The meticulous delineation of classical elements establishes a visual grammar of authority and permanence, demonstrating how formal language can encode institutional identity.
