From there, it radiates outwards to the dense array of objects and paintings on the walls and tables, following implied lines of sight from the figures and the arrangement of objects. Strong horizontal lines of tables and vertical lines of window/door frames guide the eye across the breadth and height of the composition.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is anchored by a strong rectilinear grid formed by the room's architecture: the floor, ceiling, walls, windows, and door. Within this primary grid, a secondary, more organic grid is established by the mosaic-like arrangement of framed paintings on the walls. The central figures form a triangular grouping, providing a counterpoint to the rigid architectural framework.

The left side, heavily weighted by the large window and floral arrangement, is balanced by the architectural elements (doorway, columns) and the density of paintings on the right wall. Central figures act as a fulcrum, creating a dynamic equilibrium.
