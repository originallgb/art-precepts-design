## Precept Critique
This artifact exemplifies the rigorous communication of design intent through standardized orthogonal projection and precise dimensional annotation. Its clarity demonstrates how minimal graphic elements can convey complex structural information for fabrication and assembly. The composition prioritizes functional data over aesthetic embellishment, establishing a direct visual language for industrial production.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ orthogonal projections to unambiguously communicate multi-dimensional object properties and relationships.
- Integrate critical dimensional data directly adjacent to relevant features to minimize cognitive load during interpretation.
- Utilize a high ratio of negative space to primary information, ensuring visual clarity and preventing data occlusion.
- Standardize line weights and annotation styles to differentiate structural elements from auxiliary information.
- Design for modularity and repetition, clearly indicating identical components and their spacing.
