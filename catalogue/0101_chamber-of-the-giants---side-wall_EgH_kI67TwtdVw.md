## Precept Critique
This fresco exemplifies the strategic use of visual density and dynamic asymmetry to convey overwhelming force and narrative tension. Its integration with the architectural volume, particularly the trompe l'oeil effect of collapsing walls, demonstrates a sophisticated understanding of spatial illusion and user immersion. The deliberate distortion of classical forms and the chaotic focal flow serve to disorient the viewer, effectively communicating the narrative's inherent disorder and divine retribution.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The viewer's eye is drawn into the central maelstrom of struggling figures and cascading rocks, following a chaotic, downward trajectory that emphasizes the giants' collapse. Secondary visual vectors lead upwards to the cloud-borne figures, creating a cyclical tension between the implied ascent of the divine and the descent of the punished.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is structured by a dynamic, irregular diagonal grid formed by the tumbling rock formations, converging towards the lower center to create a vortex-like effect. This underlying structure is an inverted pyramid, with a wide, chaotic base of figures and debris narrowing towards the implied ceiling, reinforcing a sense of compression.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical and dynamically imbalanced, with visual weight heavily concentrated in the lower two-thirds of the composition. This deliberate distribution of mass conveys an overwhelming force and impending collapse, with the upper cloud figures serving as a counterpoint that accentuates, rather than resolves, the disequilibrium.
