## Precept Critique
This composition exemplifies the Bauhaus principle of functional abstraction, distilling visual information to fundamental geometric primitives and primary color accents. The strategic use of transparency and intersecting diagonals establishes a dynamic spatial hierarchy, demonstrating how planar elements can imply depth without traditional perspective. Moholy-Nagy's work here provides a blueprint for constructing visual systems where clarity and tension are generated through precise formal relationships rather than representational content.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Utilize high-contrast primary accents to establish immediate focal points within an otherwise neutral or achromatic field.
- Employ overlapping transparent planes to signify hierarchical relationships or interactive states, allowing underlying elements to remain partially visible.
- Introduce diagonal elements to inject dynamism and direct visual flow, breaking the monotony of orthogonal structures.
- Define spatial depth through layering and color value shifts rather than relying solely on linear perspective.
- Limit the color palette to enhance clarity and emphasize the structural integrity of geometric forms.
