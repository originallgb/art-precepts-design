## Precept Critique
This work rigorously demonstrates the systematic exploration of color interaction within a fixed geometric module, providing a foundational study in visual perception. Its precise planar color application and stark negative space isolate the optical effects of hue, saturation, and value juxtaposition. The composition serves as an exemplary model for designing modular systems where individual components contribute to a larger, coherent, and perceptually rich whole.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ systematic color variation within a consistent geometric module to enhance perceptual understanding and demonstrate relational properties.
- Utilize significant negative space to isolate and elevate primary visual information, directing focus to core interactions.
- Establish rigid geometric frameworks to provide a stable, predictable canvas for exploring dynamic visual relationships and systematic permutations.
- Design for planar juxtaposition of colors to precisely control and demonstrate optical mixing and contrast effects without volumetric interference.
