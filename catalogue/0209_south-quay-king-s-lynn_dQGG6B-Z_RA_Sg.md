## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is initially drawn to the prominent central ship and its sails, then guided upwards along the masts into the dramatic sky, before descending to the bustling foreground activity on the quay and receding into the distant harbor on the right.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is anchored by a strong diagonal axis formed by the main ship's masts and rigging, counterbalanced by the horizontal planes of the quay and water. A triangular arrangement is suggested by the central vessel, with its base along the lower edge and its apex extending into the sky.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance is achieved through the significant visual weight of the large ship and dense foreground elements on the left, offset by the expansive, atmospheric sky and the receding, less detailed harbor on the right.
