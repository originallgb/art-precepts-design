## Precept Critique
This artifact demonstrates the critical role of precise orthographic projection in communicating complex three-dimensional forms and their functional specifications. Its clarity in dimensioning and structural representation provides an unambiguous blueprint for fabrication, underscoring the value of objective visual language in design. The iterative nature of design thinking is subtly conveyed through the progression from simplified conceptual views to detailed construction drawings.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ orthographic projections for unambiguous representation of complex objects, ensuring all critical dimensions and structural relationships are explicitly detailed.
- Utilize a clear visual hierarchy in documentation, presenting simplified overview diagrams before transitioning to granular, dimensioned construction views.
- Integrate numerical annotations directly onto visual elements to provide precise quantitative data, minimizing cognitive load for interpretation.
- Design modular components with clear connection points, as evidenced by the chair's construction, to facilitate assembly and material efficiency.
