## Precept Critique
This composition demonstrates the potent communicative power of elemental forms and primary colors, establishing clear visual hierarchy through chromatic contrast and spatial arrangement. The precise interplay of geometric planes dictates a dynamic yet stable visual field, illustrating how minimal elements can generate complex perceptual interactions. It provides a foundational lesson in leveraging figure-ground relationships and asymmetrical balance to direct attention and define spatial affordances within a constrained two-dimensional system.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Utilize high-contrast color pairings to establish immediate visual hierarchy and delineate distinct functional zones within an interface or product.
- Employ orthogonal geometric forms to create a sense of order and structural stability, even when distributing elements asymmetrically.
- Leverage negative space (dominant background) as an active compositional element that defines and amplifies the presence of positive forms and interactive components.
- Design for perceptual tension through the strategic placement of contrasting elements, guiding user focus without explicit directional cues or complex graphics.

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is initially drawn to the high-contrast blue rectangle in the upper right, then shifts to the white square in the lower left, before scanning the dominant red field. The flow is non-linear, driven by chromatic and luminance contrast.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is strictly rectilinear, based on an implied orthogonal grid. All forms are perfect rectangles or squares, aligned with the canvas edges.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance is achieved through the strategic placement and proportional weighting of the high-contrast blue and white elements against the expansive red field. Visual weight is distributed unevenly but creates equilibrium.
