## Precept Critique
This engraving exemplifies the strategic use of framing to establish visual hierarchy, directing attention from ornate contextual elements to a central architectural subject. The precise application of linear perspective and consistent light source renders complex spatial relationships with clarity, providing a blueprint for structured information display. The integration of highly decorative, organic motifs with rigid geometric planning demonstrates a design principle where controlled embellishment enhances, rather than obscures, underlying order.
