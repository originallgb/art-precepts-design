## Precept Critique
This work rigorously demonstrates how systematic formal constraints, specifically concentric squares, can generate complex perceptual phenomena through precise color interaction. It functions as a didactic tool, isolating variables of hue, saturation, and value to reveal their dynamic relationships in creating spatial illusion and visual weight. The composition's power lies in its reductionist approach, forcing the viewer to engage with the fundamental mechanics of visual perception rather than representational content.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Utilize nested geometric elements to create a strong, undeniable focal point, directing user attention inward.
- Employ a deliberate, incremental shift in color attributes (hue, saturation, value) across layered elements to imply spatial recession or advancement without relying on traditional perspective.
- Leverage significant chromatic and value contrast between adjacent elements to clearly delineate boundaries and establish visual hierarchy.
- Allow subtle textural cues (e.g., simulated canvas grain, paper fibers) to remain visible in digital representations to add perceived depth and tactile quality, even if purely visual.
- Employ perfect or near-perfect symmetry in foundational layouts to convey stability, order, and ease of navigation.
