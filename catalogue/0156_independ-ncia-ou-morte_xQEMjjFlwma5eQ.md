## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The primary focal point is Dom Pedro I, centrally positioned on a white horse with arm raised. The eye is drawn from the lower left (ox cart, commoners) up the diagonal road to the central figure, then sweeps right along the cavalry charge, creating a dynamic 'V' or 'L' shape that directs attention towards the declaration.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is anchored by a strong diagonal axis formed by the road and the cavalry, leading from the lower left to the upper right. This is reinforced by a secondary diagonal from the lower right (charging cavalry) meeting the central figure. The overall structure is a dynamic 'X' or 'V' shape, with the central figure at the apex of the 'V' formed by the converging lines of the road and the cavalry.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance. The left side features the commoners and the start of the procession, while the right side is dominated by the charging, more numerous cavalry. The central figure of Dom Pedro I acts as a fulcrum, balancing the disparate masses through his prominent position and decisive gesture.
