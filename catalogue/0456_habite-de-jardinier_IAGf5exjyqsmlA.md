## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The primary focal flow is directed to the central, anthropomorphic figure, specifically starting at the head adorned with foliage, then traversing down the body to observe the integrated tools and botanical elements. Secondary flow extends to the background, delineating the formal garden's geometric patterns.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is anchored by a strong vertical axis defined by the central figure, which itself is a composite of implied geometric forms (e.g., the rectangular torso). The background garden introduces a grid-like structure with receding linear perspective, contrasted by the checkerboard foreground plane.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The composition exhibits asymmetrical balance. The central figure is positioned axially, providing overall stability, but the distribution of tools (heavy on the right shoulder, watering can on the left) and the varied elements of the costume create a dynamic, non-mirroring equilibrium.
