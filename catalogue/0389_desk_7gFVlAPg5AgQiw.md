## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Hierarchical Layering for Functionality: Organize complex systems into distinct, visually separated layers (e.g., primary workspace, secondary storage) to guide user interaction and manage cognitive load.
- Material Contrast for Affordance: Utilize contrasting materials or colors (e.g., green felt on wood) to highlight interactive surfaces or primary functional zones within a larger structure.
- Symmetrical Balance for Stability: Employ symmetrical or near-symmetrical arrangements in foundational structures to convey reliability, formality, and ease of navigation.
- Subtle Detail for Perceived Quality: Integrate fine details (e.g., fluting, turned legs, wood grain) that, while not overtly functional, contribute significantly to the perceived craftsmanship and value of a product or interface.
- Orthogonal Grid for Structural Clarity: Base designs on a strong orthogonal grid to ensure visual order, facilitate alignment, and create a sense of robust, intentional construction.
