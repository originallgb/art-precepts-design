## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Hierarchical Density Management: When presenting complex systems, group related elements closely and use varying levels of detail to guide focus, ensuring the overall structure remains comprehensible despite high information density.
- Dynamic Structural Anchors: Employ strong diagonal and vertical lines as foundational compositional elements to create both stability and implied motion, preventing static or unbalanced visual experiences.
- Layered Information Disclosure: Utilize overlapping forms and subtle atmospheric cues to establish spatial depth, allowing for the presentation of multiple layers of information without resorting to extreme perspective distortions.
- Expressive Line Economy: Achieve material and textural fidelity through precise, varied line weights and minimal tonal washes, demonstrating that detailed rendering does not require extensive color or heavy shading.
- Contextual Annotation Integration: Integrate textual elements directly into the visual field as functional identifiers, ensuring they complement rather than disrupt the visual flow and information hierarchy.
