## Precept Critique
This work rigorously demonstrates how a simple, high-contrast geometric module, systematically repeated within a grid, can generate complex visual rhythm and define spatial boundaries. It exemplifies the concretist principle of form derived from rational structure, using diagonal elements to introduce dynamism into an otherwise static pattern. The subtle imperfections in paint application provide a critical counterpoint, humanizing the rigid geometric abstraction without compromising its underlying order.
