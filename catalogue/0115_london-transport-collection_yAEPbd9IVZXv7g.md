## Precept Critique
This composition demonstrates the efficacy of geometric reduction in conveying complex industrial forms, distilling a locomotive into its essential, recognizable components through a precise interplay of circles, rectangles, and curves. The strategic deployment of high-contrast color blocks and layered planar elements establishes a clear visual hierarchy, directing attention without relying on traditional perspective or rendering. Its enduring communicative power stems from its ability to abstract function into an iconic, memorable visual language, making complex systems immediately legible and aesthetically compelling.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Geometric Abstraction for Iconicity: Reduce complex objects or concepts to fundamental geometric shapes to create highly recognizable and scalable icons or logos.
- Layered Planar Composition: Utilize overlapping, flat color planes to establish spatial depth and visual hierarchy without employing traditional perspective or shading.
- High-Contrast Color Blocking: Employ a limited palette of high-contrast, saturated colors to define distinct functional zones or components within an interface or product.
- Modular Component Design: Design elements as self-contained, interchangeable geometric modules that can be combined and reconfigured to represent different states or configurations of a system.
- Negative Space as Structural Element: Treat negative space not merely as background, but as an active component that defines and frames positive forms, contributing to overall balance and clarity.
