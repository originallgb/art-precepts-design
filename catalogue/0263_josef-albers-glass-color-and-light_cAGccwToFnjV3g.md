## Precept Critique
This composition demonstrates the efficacy of high-contrast color blocking to establish immediate visual hierarchy and direct user attention within a sparse layout. The precise placement of geometric forms against expansive negative space exemplifies how controlled visual weight achieves dynamic balance without overt symmetry. It underscores the power of minimalist elements in conveying abstract concepts or information through pure form and color interaction.
