## Precept Critique
The precise tessellation demonstrates a granular approach to form generation, where macro-level imagery emerges from micro-level discrete units, offering a model for pixel-based rendering or modular design systems. The concentric framing and radial symmetry effectively establish a singular focal point, directing user attention with hierarchical clarity through nested visual elements. The strategic use of color variation within the limited palette of available tesserae creates volumetric illusion and textural richness, illustrating efficient resource utilization for complex visual outcomes.

- Implement a limited, curated color palette to achieve volumetric depth and textural differentiation, optimizing visual information density without chromatic overload.
