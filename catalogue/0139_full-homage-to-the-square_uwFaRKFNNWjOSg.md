## Precept Critique
This composition rigorously demonstrates how chromatic adjacency fundamentally alters perceived spatial recession and boundary definition, offering a foundational study for interface designers on visual hierarchy through color. The precise geometric framing and unmodulated color application establish a stable visual anchor, illustrating how absolute formal control can direct user focus without relying on complex graphical elements. Its systematic exploration of color interaction provides a direct heuristic for product engineers to predict perceptual shifts in material appearance based on surrounding chromatic environments.

- Utilize nested geometric forms and high-contrast color shifts to establish clear visual hierarchy and direct user attention to a central focal point.

- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is built upon a rigid, orthogonal grid defined by perfectly nested squares, establishing a highly structured and stable geometric framework.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: The artwork exhibits perfect axial and radial symmetry, resulting in a centrally balanced distribution of visual weight and a sense of formal equilibrium.
