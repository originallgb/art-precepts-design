## Precept Critique
This drawing rigorously demonstrates how one-point perspective can establish hierarchical depth and direct visual attention through successive architectural framing elements. The intricate layering of ornate details, rendered with varying wash densities, illustrates precise control over visual information to create both richness and spatial recession. Its function as a stage design underscores the principle of constructing immersive, illusionistic environments through controlled visual cues and structured spatial narratives.
