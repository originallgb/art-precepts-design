## Precept Critique
This composition exemplifies the power of planar manipulation to generate perceived three-dimensionality and dynamic tension within a two-dimensional field. The precise interplay of color blocks and sharp edges establishes a clear visual hierarchy, directing ocular movement through implied folds and spatial recession. Its formal rigor demonstrates how minimal elements, when strategically deployed, can evoke complex structural narratives and challenge perceptual assumptions.
