## Precept Critique
This composition effectively uses a high-contrast focal point against a turbulent, low-visibility environment to direct attention and establish narrative tension. The dynamic interplay of horizontal and diagonal elements, particularly in the water and sky, creates a sense of imminent motion and environmental force without relying on explicit narrative figures. The deliberate textural application of paint communicates the raw energy of the scene, translating atmospheric conditions into a tangible surface quality that enhances perceived immersion.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is drawn from the turbulent foreground waves, along the jetty to the lighthouse, then across the horizon to the distant ship, and finally up into the heavy, dynamic sky.
- **Geometric Structure**: A strong horizontal line is established by the jetty and horizon, bisected by the verticality of the lighthouse. The waves introduce diagonal and curvilinear elements, disrupting the underlying grid. The sky occupies a significant upper portion as an amorphous mass.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance is achieved through the heavy, dark sky on the left counterbalanced by the lighter, more active waves on the right. The off-center lighthouse provides a vertical anchor against the horizontal expanse.
- **Framing Density**: balanced
- **Historical Lineage**: Impressionism, Marine painting tradition
