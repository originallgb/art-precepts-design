## Precept Critique
This design exemplifies information density through minimalist abstraction, effectively communicating complex route data via simplified geometric forms and a highly constrained color palette. Its strength lies in the immediate legibility of its symbolic language, which transcends linguistic barriers to convey a clear message of expansion and connectivity. The precise alignment and proportional relationships between elements demonstrate a rigorous application of grid principles, ensuring visual coherence and functional clarity.
