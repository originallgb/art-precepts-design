## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is initially drawn to the foreground's rocky texture, then follows the horizontal line of the train and tracks towards the central station building, before expanding to the town and distant mountains. The train acts as a strong left-to-right directional vector.
- **Geometric Structure**: The composition is structured by strong horizontal bands: foreground, railway line, midground town/station, distant mountains, and sky. The station building provides a vertical anchor, breaking the horizontal rhythm and establishing a central mass. A subtle diagonal from the lower left foreground to the upper right mountain ridge adds dynamic tension.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance is achieved by positioning the prominent station building slightly to the right of center, counterbalanced by the extended length of the train and the broader expanse of the landscape on the left. Foreground elements provide distributed visual weight.
