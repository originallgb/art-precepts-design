## Precept Critique
This artifact exemplifies systematic material and chromatic investigation, demonstrating a foundational approach to design education through empirical observation and classification. Its modular grid structure and sequential color progressions provide a clear framework for understanding visual relationships, transferable to interface design for information architecture. The deliberate presentation of raw material samples underscores the Bauhaus emphasis on material honesty and the intrinsic properties of components as design drivers.
