## Precept Critique
This drawing exemplifies the power of linear perspective to construct an illusion of profound spatial depth and monumental scale within a two-dimensional plane. The systematic repetition and modularity of architectural elements, from rusticated blocks to classical columns, establish a clear structural rhythm and visual coherence.
