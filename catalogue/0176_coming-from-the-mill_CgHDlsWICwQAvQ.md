## Precept Critique
This composition effectively uses simplified, repetitive forms to convey collective identity and scale within an industrial landscape, demonstrating how abstraction can amplify thematic resonance. The deliberate flattening of perspective and uniform lighting directs focus to the human element's integration into the urban fabric, rather than individual detail. Its strength lies in establishing a pervasive atmosphere through a constrained palette and consistent visual grammar, offering a model for conveying complex narratives via minimalist means.
