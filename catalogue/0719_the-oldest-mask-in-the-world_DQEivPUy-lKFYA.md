## Precept Critique
The mask's minimalist facial features, carved into a volumetric oval, demonstrate how reductive forms can achieve profound expressive ambiguity. Its strategic use of high-contrast negative space for eyes and mouth creates immediate focal points, directing attention and implying depth without intricate detailing. The tactile granularity of the stone surface, combined with its ancient provenance, imbues the object with an inherent material honesty that transcends mere representation.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Prioritize high-contrast negative space to define critical interactive elements, ensuring immediate visual acquisition and functional clarity.
- Employ a singular, dominant form to establish primary visual hierarchy, allowing secondary details to emerge through texture and subtle volumetric shifts.
- Integrate material properties directly into the design language, allowing inherent surface qualities to communicate authenticity and historical depth.
- Utilize ambiguous facial or symbolic cues to evoke a range of emotional responses, fostering deeper user engagement through interpretive friction.
- Design for attachment or integration points (perforations) that are structurally evident and visually harmonious with the primary form, indicating potential for modularity or contextual use.
