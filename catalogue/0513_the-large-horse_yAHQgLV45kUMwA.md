## Precept Critique
This engraving exemplifies how precise linear articulation can define monumental volume and intricate texture within a constrained monochromatic palette. Dürer's masterful use of varied line density and direction provides a blueprint for rendering complex forms and material properties without relying on color or extensive spatial depth. The composition's asymmetrical balance, anchored by the horse's dominant mass, demonstrates effective visual weighting and focal point establishment for impactful communication.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The dominant mass of the horse directs the eye from its head (left) along its powerful back and haunches to the right, then down its legs. Secondary attention is drawn to the warrior's helmet and axe, providing a vertical counterpoint.
- **Geometric Structure**: The horse's body forms a robust, curvilinear mass, anchored by strong vertical and diagonal lines of its legs and the column. The background elements provide a rectilinear contrast, framing the organic forms.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance is achieved through the monumental, dense mass of the horse occupying the left and central space, counteracted by the smaller, more detailed warrior and architectural fragments on the right, preventing a complete visual collapse to the left.
