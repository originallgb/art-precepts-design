## Precept Critique
This work rigorously explores the fundamental principles of visual organization through elemental geometric forms and high-contrast values, demonstrating how systematic variation within a constrained vocabulary generates distinct perceptual experiences. It serves as a didactic exercise in visual grammar, isolating the effects of recursive patterning versus linear repetition on spatial perception and focal engagement. The piece underscores the power of abstraction to communicate complex structural ideas without relying on representational content, making it a foundational study for designers concerned with information architecture and interface clarity.
