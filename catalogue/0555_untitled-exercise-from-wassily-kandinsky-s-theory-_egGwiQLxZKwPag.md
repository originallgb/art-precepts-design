## Precept Critique
This exercise demonstrates fundamental principles of modularity and visual rhythm through the systematic arrangement of contrasting geometric units. The deliberate juxtaposition of strong chromatic values within a rigid grid structure provides a clear study in visual tension and balance. It exemplifies how basic forms and colors can generate complex perceptual effects, offering a foundational toolkit for interface design and architectural patterning.
