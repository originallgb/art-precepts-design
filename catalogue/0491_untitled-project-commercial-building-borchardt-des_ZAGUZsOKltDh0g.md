## Precept Critique
This work rigorously demonstrates the efficacy of combining orthographic and axonometric projections to communicate complex architectural concepts within a single, coherent composition. The deliberate variation in line weight effectively differentiates between established structural elements and speculative design iterations, providing a clear visual hierarchy. Its minimalist execution, utilizing only graphite on tracing paper, underscores a modernist commitment to functional clarity and essential form over superfluous detail.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ multi-view projections (e.g., elevation and axonometric) to comprehensively articulate both the functional layout and three-dimensional spatial qualities of a design within a single visual field.
- Utilize differential line weights and stroke fidelity to establish a clear visual hierarchy, distinguishing between primary structural components, secondary details, and exploratory conceptual elements.
- Leverage negative space as an active design element to define and emphasize the boundaries and forms of the primary subject, enhancing clarity and reducing visual noise.
- Prioritize geometric abstraction and essential forms in early-stage conceptualization to focus on structural integrity and spatial relationships, deferring granular detail.
