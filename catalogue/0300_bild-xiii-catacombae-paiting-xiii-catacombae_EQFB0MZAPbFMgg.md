## Precept Critique
This composition rigorously demonstrates how negative space can be actively sculpted to define primary forms and direct focal attention, rather than merely serving as background. It illustrates the power of minimal chromatic accents to establish hierarchical points of interest within an otherwise monochromatic, architectonic structure. The work provides a masterclass in abstract spatial definition, using interlocking geometric and organic elements to imply depth and passage without relying on traditional perspective.

---

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Negative Space as Primary Form: Design the void first; the surrounding elements will then define it, creating compelling spatial relationships and guiding user flow.
- Chromatic Anchoring: Employ minimal, high-contrast color accents to establish critical focal points within a largely monochromatic or low-saturation interface, drawing immediate attention to key interactive elements.
- Modular Spatial Construction: Utilize a system of interlocking geometric and curvilinear modules to build complex, yet coherent, spatial layouts that imply pathways and enclosures.
- Hierarchical Enclosure: Structure information or interactive zones by nesting elements within larger, defining forms, creating a clear sense of containment and progression.
- Implied Navigation: Design visual pathways through the arrangement of forms and negative space, subtly directing the user's eye and suggesting interaction sequences without explicit arrows or instructions.
