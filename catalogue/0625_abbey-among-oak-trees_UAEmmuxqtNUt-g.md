## Precept Critique
This composition masterfully employs negative space and silhouette to define form and direct attention, leveraging high contrast to establish a clear visual hierarchy. The strategic placement of a luminous horizon line against a dense, dark foreground creates an immersive spatial depth, guiding the viewer's gaze through layers of visual information. The integration of organic, fractal-like structures (trees) with a decaying geometric form (ruin) demonstrates a powerful method for conveying narrative tension and symbolic meaning through structural juxtaposition.

- **Geometric Structure**: A strong vertical axis is established by the central ruin and the dominant, silhouetted trees. A pronounced horizontal division separates the dark, dense foreground and midground from the expansive, lighter sky. The composition balances a central, near-symmetrical architectural element with surrounding organic, asymmetrical tree forms.
