## Precept Critique
This artifact exemplifies the integration of empirical observation with geometric abstraction to codify complex spatial data, establishing a foundational approach to information architecture. Its systematic layering of organic urban forms onto a precise radial grid demonstrates a heuristic for hierarchical data visualization. The clarity of its functional representation, achieved through distinct material applications, provides a model for differentiating data types in visual systems.

## Actionable Design Heuristics
- Employ a dominant geometric overlay (e.g., radial grid, circular frame) to provide structural coherence and navigational cues for complex, irregular data sets.
- Utilize distinct visual weights and material qualities (e.g., fine ink lines for definition, translucent washes for area) to differentiate information layers and enhance legibility.
- Integrate contextual annotations directly into the visual field, ensuring they align with the overall aesthetic and functional intent without disrupting primary information flow.
- Design for multi-scale legibility, where both macro-level patterns (city shape, river flow) and micro-level details (individual buildings, roads) are discernible within a single view.
