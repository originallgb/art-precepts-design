## Precept Critique
This work demonstrates the absolute power of fundamental geometric primitives to establish an undeniable visual hierarchy and focal point without relying on representational content. Its layered material application, combining watercolor, plaster, and collage, provides a tactile counterpoint to its intellectual abstraction, offering lessons in subtle textural differentiation within a minimalist framework. The precise yet imperfect execution of concentric forms reveals the inherent tension between ideal geometric concepts and their physical manifestation, a critical consideration for both digital rendering and physical prototyping.
