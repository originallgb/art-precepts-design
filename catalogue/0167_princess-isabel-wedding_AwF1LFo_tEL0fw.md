## Precept Critique
This composition masterfully employs deep perspective and hierarchical scaling to direct attention, establishing a clear visual narrative within a complex spatial environment. The strategic deployment of saturated, warm colors in the foreground against a cooler, more diffuse background effectively segments information and emphasizes focal points. Its intricate layering of architectural elements and human forms demonstrates a robust system for managing visual density without sacrificing clarity or depth.

## The 5 Evaluative Angles

### 1. Composition & Lineage
- **Focal Flow**: The eye is drawn immediately to the kneeling couple and the officiating priest in the lower-center, then follows the architectural lines upwards and along the rows of spectators, eventually leading to the distant, brighter background. A strong diagonal from the upper left curtain to the lower right figures creates dynamic tension.
- **Geometric Structure**: Dominated by a strong central vertical axis implied by the gap in the curtains and the distant aisle, intersected by a horizontal plane established by the carpet and the kneeling figures. Diagonal lines from the architectural elements (arches, cornices) and the draped curtains create a sense of depth and grandeur, leading to a vanishing point in the upper-middle distance.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Asymmetrical balance. The central figures anchor the composition, but the left side is heavier with the large curtain and a denser group of figures, while the right side features a more open architectural view and tiered spectators. The overall visual weight is distributed to maintain equilibrium despite the lack of strict bilateral symmetry.
